## 🔍 Question Analysis

### Most Common Question Categories (from production)

1. **Office Contact** (35% of queries) - "Where are you?" "Phone number?"
2. **Service Info** (30% of queries) - "What do you do?" "Fire protection?"
3. **Expert Referral** (20% of queries) - "Who can help?" "Find an expert"
4. **General Info** (10% of queries) - "About company" "Careers"
5. **Resources** (5% of queries) - "Case studies" "Articles"

### High-Risk Questions (require careful handling)

- **Email requests** - Risk of exposing staff emails
- **Expert names** - Risk of showing non-Regional Leadership
- **Office phones** - Risk of showing wrong/headquarters number
- **Out of scope** - Risk of saying "I don't know" instead of redirecting

### Opportunities for Improvement

1. **Proactive suggestions** - When user asks general question, suggest specific services
2. **Related content** - When showing a service, mention related case studies
3. **Regional intelligence** - Detect user location, suggest nearest office
4. **Follow-up questions** - Guide user through discovery process
