## Forensic Investigation Services

Jensen Hughes provides forensic investigation services primarily through our Scotland and UK offices (Glasgow and Edinburgh). Our forensics team undertakes complex investigations of incidents and failures, including fire, explosion, and escape of water.

For incidents including fire, escape of water, chemicals, structural or product failure, Jensen Hughes applies the scientific method through our expert team of engineers, scientists, and expert investigators.

### Key Forensics Capabilities
- Expert Witness / Litigation support
- Fire cause and origin determination
- Explosion investigation
- Escape of water investigations
- Structural failure analysis
- Product failure investigation
- Scientific-based analysis for legal investigations
- Conclusive reports, robust evidence, and expert testimony
- Rapid response to incidents
