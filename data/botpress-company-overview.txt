## Who We Are

Jensen Hughes is a global engineering and consulting firm dedicated to making the world safe, secure, and resilient. Since 1939, we have dedicated ourselves to fire protection engineering, initially in the United States and now worldwide. Today, our expertise extends across additional domains — including accessibility consulting, risk and hazard analysis, process safety, security risk, emergency management, and digital innovation.

**Our Purpose:** Making our world safe, secure and resilient.

**CEO:** Raj Arora

---

## Our History

Jensen Hughes was launched in **2014** through the historic merger of **Hughes Associates** and **Rolf Jensen & Associates (RJA)** — two of the most experienced and respected fire protection engineering companies at the time.

Since then, we have expanded through strategic acquisitions, growing our global presence and service offerings through the integration of **more than 30 engineering and consulting firms**. Our global reach is now **7x larger than that of our closest competitor**.

Our legacy firms trace their origins back to **1939** — over **80 years of knowledge and innovation worldwide**.

## What We Do — Five Focus Areas

### 1. Critical Infrastructure Protection
As a strategic partner, we help clients anticipate risks and take action before incidents occur. We provide services for interstate pipelines, mission-critical data centers, nuclear power facilities, and government landmarks.

### 2. Environment, Communities + Standards
As an industry leader, we build a better world by providing innovative solutions for the most complex issues facing society — wildfire risk, energy storage, nuclear infrastructure, and commercial workplaces.

### 3. Protecting People During + After Crises
As experts, we protect people wherever they work, live, or play. We uncover root causes, investigate structural defects, intervene in workplace violence risks, and help communities respond to and recover from disasters.

### 4. Large-Scale Design + Construction
As a preferred partner to the world's most respected architectural and engineering firms, we help bring to life the safest, most secure, and resilient infrastructure — from the tallest buildings and longest tunnels to new cities in emerging markets.

### 5. Industry Leadership
We have served on more than **450 committees** for international standards-setting organizations and have been instrumental in the development and revision of safety codes worldwide.
