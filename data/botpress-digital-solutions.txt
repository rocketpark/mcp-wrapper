## HazAdvisr™ — Real-Time Hazardous Materials Compliance + Inventory Management

HazAdvisr helps organizations manage hazardous materials with confidence across sites through a real-time, centralized platform. Built for EH&S, lab, and operations teams, it supports chemical inventory classification, storage thresholds, and occupancy-based limits.

**Compliance Standards Supported:** ICC, NFPA, FM Global, and other jurisdictional requirements

**Key Capabilities:**
- Upload chemicals, track properties and hazard classifications
- Identify potential hazards and proactively address issues
- Create building layouts and add chemical inventories to determine Maximum Allowable Quantities (MAQs)
- Evaluate whether facilities exceed MAQs in control areas, lab suites, high-hazard spaces
- Drag-and-drop planning module for rapid iteration
- Export regulatory compliance reports
- Multi-site environment management

**Why Use It:**
- Improve Compliance: Navigate complex regulatory and insurance requirements
- Minimize Risk: Full visibility into chemical hazards with Jensen Hughes expert support
- Design Efficiently: Quickly check compliance before finalizing building designs

**Best For:** Science + technology, pharmaceutical, research labs, manufacturing, multi-site EH&S teams
