## Fallback rule for bot

If a user types an acronym not in this list AND the rest of the KB doesn't match it:
1. Ask once for the expansion ("Could you tell me what BESS stands for in your context?").
2. If they answer, search again with the expansion.
3. If they don't, link the regional /services landing page and offer info@jensenhughes.com.

Never reply "I don't recognize that acronym" without offering a fallback URL.
