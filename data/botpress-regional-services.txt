# Jensen Hughes Regional Service Availability + URL Reference

Last updated: 2026-05-11
Source: Live jensenhughes.com per-region sitemaps

Each region has its own URL prefix AND its own service taxonomy/slugs. Service slugs differ between regions — do NOT assume the global slug works under a regional prefix.

---

## Slug Routing Rule

1. If you know the user's region AND the exact regional slug for the service they asked about, use the deep URL.
2. If you DO NOT know the exact regional slug, link ONLY to the region's services landing page (e.g., /europe/services). Do not guess a slug.
3. NEVER assume a global slug works for a regional URL — many slugs differ (Europe uses "fire-engineering-consultancy", Pacific uses "fire-engineering", Global uses "fire-engineering-systems-design").
